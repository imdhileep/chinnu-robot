🥽 Chinnu VR

  • Look around — Chinnu's head follows your head
  • Left stick — walk: up forward, down back, left/right turn
  • Right stick up — walk where you are looking (Chinnu turns its body to your look, then walks)
  • Right stick down — walk backward
  • A — recenter (look straight, press A)
  • B — head-follow on / off
  • Right trigger — Chinnu wags tail
  • X (left) — Chinnu screen size: small / big / hidden
  • Left trigger (hold) — talk to Chinnu; the answer plays in your headset
